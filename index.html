Skyler Hensley

Skyler Hensley is the author behind TEAM HQ. With a growing collection of stories and approximately fifteen books already written, Skyler is building a world of mystery, adventure, danger, and imagination one story at a time.

TEAM HQ is the publishing headquarters for those stories — beginning with the Arrow Jade Series followed by the highly anticipated Ace Shard Series and more adventures to come!

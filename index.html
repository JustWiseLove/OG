About TEAM HQ

TEAM HQ is the publishing home of the A TEAM Series. TEAM stands for Talented Experts Analyzing Mysteries.

These stories celebrate family, courage, and the thrill of uncovering the truth. Written for readers who love action, adventure, and mysteries that keep you guessing — with strong family dynamics at the heart of every mission.
